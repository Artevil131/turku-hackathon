The surface is a distance-weighted smoothing of the 23 county estimates (newly found carriers per 100,000 per year), so it is a picture of pressure, not a measurement between towns; the numbers live on the stepped map. Dashed lines are contours of the same surface. Arrows run along hospital referral links from the higher-rate end to the lower, width proportional to the difference, which is the flow term of a diffusion model on the referral network. Dots are hospitals: large are the five university hospitals, small the county central hospitals; the pulses along the links are decoration.
THL Tartuntatautirekisteri (fact_ttr_cases), Statistics Finland polygons, both CC BY 4.0. Newest months may be incomplete.
