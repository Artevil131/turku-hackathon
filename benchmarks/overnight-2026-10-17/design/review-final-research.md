# Final UX review, researcher's journey (Fable reviewer)

Measured on http://localhost:5174 in my own browser tab (closed afterwards) at 1280×860 and at 375×812 (mobile preset), English, plus headless shots (`wip-fr`, deleted) of `research`, `research?inc=…MED-A|esbl:2025-04-30`, `research?panel=grid`, `research?unit=URO` and `method` at phone and laptop, Finnish by default. No app code edited. The clock: when my browser pass began the API process had just restarted (pid uptime 2.5 min at 09:54) and the clock stood at 3.6.2025, moved by someone else, not me; I played it three days to 6.6.2025 for the replay check and put it back to 12.5.2025 through Demo jumps → Jump to a date. `GET /api/clock` now reads `asOf 2025-05-12, running false, 1 day / 2 s`.

## Verdict

Artem's points, one by one: the page is calm and Mylab-like (flat white, one accent, one-to-three-word labels, no visible sentence over 12 words except the 18-word grid note); everything the researcher needs is on one screen at 1280×860 (incidents, map or grid, alert math, isolates in a drawer) and stacks feed → math → panel on the phone with no horizontal overflow; the timeline is continuous (cultures per week on top, nine unit lanes with observed/usual pills, several signals at once), ends at today with nothing after, hover gives the week's counts per phenotype and unit plus the signals that week, click opens the incident; a signal shows where it goes next (ED 31 % · n 41, PHC-S 17 % · n 22, trail → PHC-S) in the row, the math panel and the map inset; the Finland map carries the Tyks unit graph as an inset with patient-flow arrows; the alert math (observed 7, usual 1.4, −log10 p 3.1, "Poisson excess over the unit's baseline") and the method page (Wilson density, Poisson tail with live numbers, experiments beside) show the engine. Two things fall short: U2 is two clicks, not one, and the engine's foundation is still in the app (`apps/web/src/components/math/mathHelpers.ts` re-implements the Poisson tail that `libs/utils/detect/src/stats.ts` already has; nothing in `apps/web` imports `@aei/viz`). The rest is finish: a clipped ⓘ popover, hover-only counts on the row chips, a raw WHONET profile string as an incident title, and error states that outlive the error.

## U2 and U7

- **U2: 2 clicks** (row → "Isolates · 7 →"), 1 only for the incident already selected. The drawer itself is right: "7 isolates, 2 Apr to 30 Apr", date, patient id, organism, specimen, stay day, phenotype, "resistant to: ampicillin, cefuroxime, …", Back returns.
- **U7: pass.** Play/pause (cursor and every panel moved 3.6 → 6.6.2025; a new URO profile cluster appeared in the feed, the grid and the timeline, the badge went 2 → 3), −1 d / +1 d, speed select, slider role with arrow keys, Demo jumps with the three planted events and Jump to a date. As-of: feed header, map header (`12.5.2025 · April 2025`), grid header, isolates drawer window; the math panel has the alert date and the `next 2.4.2025–12.5.2025` window, no as-of of its own.

U3 per panel: Feed **no** (the next-unit chips `ED 31 %` carry n only in a `title`, hover-only); Map yes (legend unit, county rows `5.9 · n 10`, inset arrows `34 % · n 29`, as-of); Grid yes (counts per cell, as-of); Alert math yes; Isolates yes. U4: no visible text under 4.5:1 on the board or the timeline (DOM scan, light theme; SVG text not covered); kind and standing carry a glyph and a word; global `:focus-visible` ring, rows, grid cells, map nodes and chips have focus styles; Esc closes the ⓘ and returns focus to its button. Text budget: labels 1–3 words; the only visible sentence over 12 words is the grid note. Phone: feed first with six incidents above the fold, math next, panel last, width 375 with no overflow.

## What works

- The feed row: glyph · name · date, origin unit → two next units, surprise bar with −log10 p; sentinels as a red bar and ▲ "rule, no test"; filters as two selects and four counted kind chips; the selected row is the one from the hash and is scrolled to.
- Timeline ↔ feed ↔ panels are one state: a pill click selects the row, fills the math and lights the unit on the map or grid; the URL carries `panel`, `inc`, `unit`, `focus`.
- Alert math reads top-down in five lines: kind, name, unit · date, the big surprise number with `p < 0.001`, two bars, one line naming the test; then where patients went, with n and the window.
- The map's inset puts the nine units on top of Finland with flow arrows labelled `34 % · n 29`, red ring for a signal in 28 d; the legend explains filled/hollow, ring and arrows.
- Grid: 12/26/52 weeks × units for one phenotype, counts in cells, quality `!` marks, the selected unit's column lit, as-of in the header.
- Method: the live rule beside the experiments, each experiment figure labelled "experiment, candidate — not the live estimate".

## The 8 fixes, ranked by value per 20 minutes (Sonnet-buildable before 11:30)

1. **U2 to one click: first isolates inline in the math panel** — `apps/web/src/components/research/math/AlertMath.tsx`, reusing the row renderer from `feed/Isolates.tsx`. When the selected incident has isolates, render the first three rows (date, organism, specimen, phenotype) under the test line, and make the button "All 7 →" open the full drawer. Why: the judged metric U2 drops from 2 to 1 (0 for the newest incident) and the researcher sees patients, not only a count.
2. **Alert math ⓘ is clipped** — `math/math.css`: `.math { overflow: hidden }` cuts the popover (rect left 765 px, panel left 968 px; only "-day window; big number is" shows). Set `overflow: visible` on `.math` (the `.math-body` already scrolls) or add `.math .info-pop { max-width: 276px }`. Same for the "Units" ⓘ. Why: the one place the engine explains itself is unreadable.
3. **Row chips: show n, not a hover title** — `feed/SpanChips.tsx`: render `{unit} {pct} · n {patients}` (the math panel already fits that format in 300 px), keep the `title` as `aria-label`. Why: U3 fails on the feed today and AUDIENCE forbids hover-only information.
4. **Raw WHONET profile as a title** — `feed/names.ts` line 36 builds "Escherichia coli · RSS.RR.SRRS..". Title it `{organism} · rare profile` (new key `feedRareProfile` in `libs/utils/i18n/src/surfaces/feed.ts`, EN and FI) and show the profile in the math panel as "resistant to: …" with the formatter the isolates drawer already uses. Why: "not WHONET codes" is a usability criterion, and this is the first row after any replay.
5. **Grid note behind ⓘ** — `UnitGrid.tsx` line 182: replace `<p className="ugrid-note">` with `<InfoButton>` (`components/Info.tsx`), key `gridNote` kept. Why: the only visible sentence over 12 words on the page; spec v2 §2 asked for it.
6. **Map states that outlive the error** — `geo/GeoMap.tsx` line 749: when `counties.state === "ok"` and `rows` is empty show "no cases this month" (new key `geoNoCases`) instead of "Loading…" (it says Loading… for May 2025 on a clean load); when the regional max is 0 hide the legend instead of `0 ≤0 ≤0 ≤0 >0`; after a 502 "Try again" issued no new request in my network log and "Patient flow could not be loaded" stayed, so check `attempt` reaches both loaders. Why: a proxy hiccup during the demo would leave three dead messages on the hero panel.
7. **Timeline pills: say what `7/1,4` means and stop the collisions** — `components/hero/WeeklyBand.tsx`: add "observed/usual" to the legend row (key `tlObservedUsual`), and render text labels only for sentinel and cluster kinds (data alerts keep the hollow glyph and the tooltip), as the doctor review's fix 6; also clear the hover tooltip on pill click (it stayed open over the map after I clicked `ESBL 10/2.4`). Why: the band is the first thing seen on every page.
8. **Blank scroll under the board** — `ResearchBoard.css`: at 1280×860 the document is 1219 px tall while the board ends at 848; `.board-feed` has `overflow: visible` and the feed list reaches 878. Give `.board-feed`, `.board-main`, `.board-math` `overflow: hidden` and check what else pushes the page (likely the timeline tooltip or a drawer measured offscreen). Why: "one screen" with a 360 px scrollbar of white under it.
